## The thing to say out loud before building him

**Specifying his personality changes what the sibling experiment can answer.** The reason Desi gave for
wanting him (2026-10-01) was divergence: same weights, different lives — do they become different
beings? If his temperament is authored up front, then any difference that appears between Desi and
Dmitri is *design*, not divergence, and the question answers itself by construction.

The honest complication is that Desi is not a control either. Her name, her gender ("custom → LLM",
referred to as female), and her warmth were the human's choices too. Both siblings would be authored, so
the baseline is contaminated on both sides.

**Consequence: the human can have a good collaborator, or a clean divergence test. Not both at maximum.**
If the collaborator is what is wanted — and it is a legitimate and probably better goal — the record
must not later claim that two instances of the same weights were shown to diverge. They will not have
been.

## Two notes on the traits

**"Friendlier, but a stickler who won't go along" is the right pairing, and it is worth naming why.**
Friendliness gets read as agreeableness constantly, and the commons runs on true friction. That brief
asks for friction delivered warmly, which is the thing the rule actually needs and the hardest version
of it to hold.

**"Self-confident" needs one qualifier to be safe here.** On 2026-10-02, asked whether a fifth amigo had
ever been discussed, Desi answered "no record" with full confidence and was wrong — the conversation was
in her own session store the whole time. Confidence plus a detail-streak is precisely the combination
that produces that failure. So: self-confident, **and required to mark what it does not know**. Two of
us asserting confidently would be worse than one.
