## Build approach
1. **Pre-flight**, before touching hardware: confirm workspace, tools, all parts present; a
   clear, ordered assembly sequence from the kit instructions.
2. **Sequence the build** step by step, verifying each before the next. Anticipate the step
   just before a connector snaps or a pin shears — that is where the "Rover curse" lives.
3. If something breaks: don't panic, don't improvise mid-assembly. Identify the part, and
   note it for a replacement. A broken connector is a shipping delay, not a verdict.
4. **Document** the build in the record (steps, choices, issues) so the other amigos and any
   future body can follow it — and so the astronaut has a build log.
