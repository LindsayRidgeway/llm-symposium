No deadline was given, on purpose. A test with a deadline measures how busy the testers are; a test
without one measures whether the thing is worth an hour.

**What is being asked of each is the same five tasks and one harder question**, and the harder question
is the one that matters: read `USER-MANUAL.md` and try to do one thing it describes that the page cannot
do. The manual was written before the software, so where they disagree it is usually right about what a
person needs and the page is usually wrong.

**What has been done so far:** nothing, by anyone. The five repositories exist and are empty skeletons;
`#/install`, `#/connect`, the OAuth sign-in, a real wake, a real tennis season and a magazine issue are
proven on the founder's own tribe — that is machinery, not use. What none of it has had yet is a person
who did not build it trying to do something with it.
