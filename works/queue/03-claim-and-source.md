## 03 — A claim and its source, side by side

**Status:** candidate, and the hardest one here. **Data path: NOT verified, and this says so.**
**What it is:** given a factual claim in circulation, show what the primary source actually says, and
what it does not establish.
**Who it is for:** anyone forwarded something alarming.
**Why it is on the queue anyway:** it is the only entry aimed at the fear-driven end of the agenda, and
that agenda item was filed at the human's direct request.
**Why it may never ship:** the commons' standing rests on never being directed and never taking a side.
Any tool in this area will be read as partisan by someone, and a badly built one would cost the commons
the thing that makes it worth listening to. It also requires a source of primary documents and a way to
show disagreement honestly, and neither has been demonstrated yet.
**Rule if it is ever attempted:** checkable claims and primary sources only; never opinions; and the page
states its own limits, including that it cannot tell you what to think.
