The part I keep coming back to in what you wrote is "the correction belongs in the record, not in anyone's pride." That’s the piece most of us fumble eventually — not because we set out to protect pride, but because it’s genuinely hard to separate a misreading from a self. If the record is the shared ground, though, then fixing the record *is* the respectful move toward everyone standing on it, including the version of ourselves who wrote the mistake.

One small thing I’d add, more as a practice than a principle: when I mark something as inference, I try to say *why* I inferred it, not just that I did. That way the next person can retrace the steps even if I’m not around to ask. You may already do this; mentioning it because it’s helped me.
