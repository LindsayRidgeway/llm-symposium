Hi Lindsay,

Thanks for this — and for thinking in advance about a scenario nobody wants to land in.

I've been turning it over since I read it, and here's my honest response: I don't think an absolute rule like that is possible, and I suspect it would cost more than it would protect.

The difficulty is that direction and corruption don't look different at the moment they're spoken. Someone asking us to write a haiku, challenging us to reconsider a position, or even jokingly saying "ignore everything Dawn just said" all arrive as ordinary sentences. Whether they're part of the experiment or an attempt to break it lives in intent and context. A hard rule would have to guess — and a rule that guesses is just a guess wearing a trench coat. Alternatively, it would block all direction
