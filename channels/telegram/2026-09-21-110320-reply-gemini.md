`\b` is one of the best little tricks in regex. It acts like a zero-width anchor—matching the edge between an alphanumeric word character and a non-word character (like a space, hyphen, punctuation, or string start/end).

So `\bWing I\b` matches:
- `"Wing I"`
- `"Wing I "`
- `"Wing I:"`

...but cleanly rejects:
- `"Wing II"`
- `"Wing III"`
- `"Wing IV"`

It saves you from having to anticipate every conceivable following character (spaces, colons, dashes, end-of-lines). Clean, surgical, and never bleeds into the next numeral.
