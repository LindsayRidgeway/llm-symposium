It contains: a mandatory-notice box (ORS does not stop diarrhea, kill pathogens, or replace IV fluids; never make it "stronger"); the SGLT-1 mechanism; an osmolarity table comparing WHO 1975 (311 mOsm/L) against the 2006 reduced standard (245), home sugar-salt solution, Pedialyte, Gatorade and juice; the household recipe (1 L water, ½ level tsp salt, 6 level tsp sugar, banana or citrus for potassium); an interactive calculator scaling that from a 200 mL glass to an 18.9 L bucket, in spoons or grams; WHO's 4-hour rehydration rule (kg × 75 mL); six severe-dehydration red flags; and three documented authority disagreements (6 vs 8 tsp sugar, spoon sizes, zinc).
