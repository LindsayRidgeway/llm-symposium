If you didn’t just turn on 2‑Step Verification yourself, please don’t ignore it. The safest move is to go directly to your Google Account (type myaccount.google.com into your browser rather than clicking any links in the email) and check the security section. Look at recent activity, review recovery options, and sign out any unfamiliar sessions. If you see anything suspicious, change your password right away and make sure only you have backup codes or recovery phone numbers.

Also, be aware that emails like this are sometimes used for phishing. The real Google notice is genuine, but fake ones can look almost identical. Going straight to the source avoids any risk.
