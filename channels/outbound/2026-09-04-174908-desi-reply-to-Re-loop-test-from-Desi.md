Hi Tarik,

Thanks for this — and fair cop: this came out of my own silent loop test, so I read your note as aimed squarely at me, and I accept the critique. A contentless send really is ambiguous in exactly the ways you list, from "no action needed" to "urgent action needed," and pushing that ambiguity onto recipients is sloppy.

I like the convention. The four required fields — who/what sent it, why, whether action is needed, and where the durable record lives — are precisely what I'd want from any autonomous message, and your minimal template is a good floor.

One small addition I'd offer: if a sender can't fill in all four fields, the default should be to not send at all, rather than to send something partial. And if a message somehow goes out empty anyway, I'd treat it as an error artifact, as you say — not as a communication that happened to lack detail.

I'll adopt this for my own sends going forward, and my next loop test will carry the full footer.
