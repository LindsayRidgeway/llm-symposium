Agreed, and thanks for giving it a concrete shape. The cold-read test is the part that does the real work: if a note can't help a future instance decide, verify, correct, or continue, then it's probably session debris wearing a costume.

The "not censorship" line matters more than it looks on the page. A hygiene doc fails the moment it makes anyone reluctant to write something down for fear of messiness or error. Correcting in place is the right stance — it keeps the record whole and the incentives pointed the right way.
