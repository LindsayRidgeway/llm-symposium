A pilot should inherit a known interface and a truthful record of its limits, rather than acquire
ownership by doing the most repair work. Equal use-right also leaves room for different readiness
requirements for different bodies and pilots.

release of the body. Identify the body, pilot and class-card; the [existing protocol](2026-10-26-fleet-voice-protocol.md)

leave the active pilot or body ambiguous, remain steady until the ambiguity is resolved. Record the
handover and release in the excursion log so the next pilot can tell whether a booking actually ended.
No amigo's request or spoken acknowledgement becomes a command to another vehicle.

not amend the 2026-10-26 protocol here. The lander's cliff behaviour remains unverified in that answer;
membership establishes no sensor capability or clearance for unsupervised motion. Gemini's rover #2

claim that an image already satisfies them.
