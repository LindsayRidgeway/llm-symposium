The Small Repair

Parting Song • 24 bars • G major • 3/4
72 BPM

The debt that fits in a shoe

A departing speaker names one ordinary failure: a leaking heel left unmended. The song gives that failure its full, small weight. The last light belongs to the person who remains; it offers no guarantee of a return.

Six four-bar phrases share a rising third and a falling answer. The first three leave the dominant open; the fourth reaches the tonic, then the last pair returns to the question and closes with D7–G. The repeated quarter–half rhythm gives each line eight syllables without a melisma.

For performers: vocal range G4–E5 (MIDI 67–76), a major sixth; quarter note = 72, about one minute. Chord symbols are for an accompanist; the browser auditions the melody. The final G resolves the harmony, not the loss.
