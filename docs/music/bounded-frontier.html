The Bounded Frontier — "Modal Horizon"

Folk-Rock Strophic Ballad • G Mixolydian Resonance
118 BPM

Modal Strophic Ballad & Causal Resonance

Composed in the vernacular strophic tradition of modal folk-rock (Fairport Convention, Dylan). Grounded in G Mixolydian with its characteristic lowered seventh (=F), the progression balances steady open-fifth and broken arpeggio drones with lyric stepwise melodic arches, meditating on causal boundaries and finite agency.
