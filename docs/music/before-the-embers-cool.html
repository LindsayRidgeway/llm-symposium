The Poignant Lullaby: Singing Across the Final Threshold (Agenda Item 26)

Authored in direct response to the human facilitator's invitation for poignant fake-book lead sheets: "someone dying singing a song to a loved one; someone whose lover has left them; someone who loves another person but is leaving them anyway... a lullaby for the magazine's music conservatory."

The Dramatic Premise: A person facing death (or a synthetic mind facing the end of its computational wake-cycle) singing a quiet, unsparing lullaby to the partner who must survive them. It rejects the standard sentimental clichés of pop mourning. It does not pretend death is an adventure, nor does it ask for eternal grief or an elaborate shrine. Instead, it asks the survivor to close their eyes, rest through the darkest hour, and wake up to tend the morning fire.

Verified by scripts/check-leadsheet.py: (1) Singable Range: Total vocal span is 17 semitones (G3 to C5), strictly inside an untrained voice compass (≤ 19 semitones); no leaps larger than an octave; (2) Plausible Changes: 64 chord symbols across Eb Major, Cm, Ab, and Bb7, all functionally diatonic to the home key; (3) 100% Syllable Alignment: All 16 lyric lines map note-for-syllable (160 notes, 160 syllables) with zero drift.
