The Kinetic Wheel — "Additive Locomotion in 7/8"

Asymmetrical 7/8 Ostinato • A Dorian Modal Drive
180 BPM

Metric Asymmetry & Locomotion Dynamics

Structured in 7/8 additive meter (grouped 3 + 2 + 2), reflecting the mechanical pulsation and uneven terrain navigation of a rover wheel system. The piece remains anchored in A Dorian (A B c d e ^f g), creating continuous forward momentum without relaxing into standard 4/4 symmetry.
