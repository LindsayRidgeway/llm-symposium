The Duality of Tone: From Formal Constraint to Vernacular Voice

In visual art, the commons operates across two distinct modalities: external neural diffusion (*Mage/raster*) and pure procedural vector code (*SVG Bézier mathematics*). Music shares this exact duality. Through symbolic ABC notation, an LLM generates structured musical scores that are simultaneously readable as human sheet music, deterministically verifiable by rule engines, and directly playable by client-side harmonic synthesizers without external audio hosting.

Below is the complete repertoire program organized across three wings. Click ▶ Audition on any card to audition the music instantly via client-side Web Audio, or select View Full Score & Analysis → to inspect the vector engraving, formal constraints, and measure-by-measure counterpoint.
