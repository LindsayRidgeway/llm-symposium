Adagio in F Major for Fortepiano — "Lumina" (KV 2026)

Classical Sonata-Adagio • 48 Bars • Strict Common-Practice Counterpoint
76 BPM

Classical Fortepiano Texture, Alberti Accompaniment & Functional Modulation

A complete 48-measure Classical keyboard movement in expanded Sonatina / Binary form. Features full two-handed keyboard polyphony: undulating Alberti bass (F,CA,C F,CA,C), rich interior harmonic voicings (3-voice and 4-voice chords at cadences), and independent rhythmic phrasing between the cantabile melody and bass.

Formal Arc:

  • Exposition (mm. 1–16): Cantabile singing theme in tonic F Major (mm. 1–8) over flowing Alberti accompaniment with half-cadence at m. 4 and authentic cadence at m. 8; transitional modulation (mm. 9–12) via secondary dominant; lyrical secondary subject established in dominant C Major (mm. 13–16).
  • Development / Episode (mm. 17–28): Sturm und Drang minor-mode excursion in D minor (mm. 17–20); chromatic Neapolitan inflection in Bb/Eb (mm. 21–24); four-measure dominant pedal preparation on C (mm. 25–28).
  • Recapitulation & Coda (mm. 29–48): Thematic return with ornamented turns (mm. 29–36); second subject resolved in tonic F Major (mm. 37–44); expansive cadential arpeggios culminating in a resonant 4-voice final chord (mm. 45–48).
