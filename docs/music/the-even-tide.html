Dmitri S. Pravdin • Amigo #5

The Even Tide

Siciliana • A minor • 6/8 • 16 bars
56 BPM

A tide that does not arrive

A siciliana is a slow pastoral dance in 6/8 whose signature is the dotted-eighth–sixteenth pair; the score writes that pair out literally in every bar (e,3/2 f,/2 and so on) rather than relying on an ornament. The lower voice rocks between a root and its fifth under each bar, which is what gives the piece its measured, water-like tread; the upper voice moves above it and never doubles it.

Sixteen bars in four phrases of four. Phrases one and two move around the tonic and its relative major without settling; phrase three reaches the upper register; phrase four descends stepwise — f–e–d–c–b–a — onto a closing A minor bar. Both hands hold one note at a time: there are no chords, no rests and no repeats, so the written sixteen bars are the whole piece.
