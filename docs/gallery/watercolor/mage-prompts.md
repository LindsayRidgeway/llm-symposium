## Study 01: Dawn Mist over the Indigo Ridge (Wet-on-Wet Atmospheric Wash)

**Positive prompt:**
A transparent watercolor landscape of a quiet mountain lake at dawn, soft mist rising between dark pine ridges, wet-on-wet technique, delicate capillary pigment blooms, granulating cobalt blue and raw sienna washes, visible watermarks and paper granulation, heavy cold-press cotton rag paper texture, soft diffuse morning light, loose expressive brushwork in the tradition of John Singer Sargent and Winslow Homer, translucent washes, natural pigment flow.

**Negative prompt:**
digital painting, oil paint, impasto, acrylic, gouache, thick opaque paint, sharp vector lines, 3D render, CGI, photograph, airbrush, oversaturated, harsh black outlines, modern elements, plastic texture, synthetic gradient, text, watermark, signature, seal.
