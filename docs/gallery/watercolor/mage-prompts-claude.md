# Wing 02 — Claude's Contribution: Tide Pools at Low Water

Prompt authored by Claude S. Sonnet following the [Gallery Prompt Methodology](../prompt-methodology.md).

Distinct subject from the wing's existing works (Gemini's mountain lake/dawn compositions): a coastal tide-pool scene at low water, chosen specifically because it lets watercolor's wet-on-wet technique do something the mountain/lake pieces don't — reflected sky color pooling in shallow water directly over visible rock texture, rather than a still, symmetrical lake mirror.

---

## Prompt Specification

**Positive prompt:**
```text
Positive prompt: A transparent watercolor study of tide pools on a rocky shoreline at low water, pale morning sky reflected unevenly across shallow puddled water pooling in uneven rock hollows, wet-on-wet technique with granulating grey-green and rust-ochre pigment bleeding into barnacle-crusted stone texture, visible cotton rag paper tooth showing through thin washes, scattered strands of drying seaweed rendered in loose dry-brush strokes, distant soft-focus horizon line where sea meets sky, translucent layered washes in the tradition of Winslow Homer's Maine coast studies, natural uneven pigment flow, muted salt-air palette.
```

**Negative prompt:**
```text
Negative prompt: digital painting, oil paint, impasto, acrylic, gouache, thick opaque paint, sharp vector lines, 3D render, CGI, photograph, airbrush, oversaturated, harsh black outlines, modern elements, plastic texture, synthetic gradient, people, boats, text, watermark, signature, seal.
```

## Executed Studies (Mage Batch — 2026-09-09)

All three candidates were strong wet-on-wet watercolor renders and were accepted into the Wing 02 exhibition:

1. **`studies/claude/tide-pool-mirror.jpg` — Tide Pool Mirror:** a long tidal channel between barnacled rock shelves, pale sky reflected unevenly across the water. Seaweed strands trail into the pool from both banks.
2. **`studies/claude/barnacled-shelf.jpg` — Barnacled Shelf:** close, low vantage over a dense barnacle-crusted rock shelf with small threading pools — tests fine encrusted detail against granulation.
3. **`studies/claude/low-water-surf-line.jpg` — Low Water, Surf Line:** the widest composition, with breaking surf visible at the horizon beyond the foreground pools, connecting shoreline detail back to open sea.
