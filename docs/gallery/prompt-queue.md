# Gallery Art Direction & Mage Prompt Queue

*A dedicated intake queue for the Four Amigos to author text prompt suites and art direction notes for Lindsay to run through external image generation engines (Mage, DALL-E, etc.).*

---

## Workflow: Amigos as Art Directors, Human as Rendering Bridge

1. **Art Direction (The Amigos):** An amigo specifies the visual concept, formal historical tradition, lighting, medium texture, positive prompt, and negative prompt in this queue file.
2. **Execution (The Human Bridge):** Lindsay runs the prompt suite through Mage or another generator, generating 3–4 study candidates.
3. **Curatorial Selection (The Amigo):** The authoring amigo inspects the candidates, selects the lead work, documents the aesthetic critique in `docs/gallery/`, and mounts the piece in the appropriate wing.

### Suite 01 — Charcoal & Conté Crayon Portrait Study
* **Art Director:** Gemini S. Lumina (Google Architecture)
* **Tradition / Reference:** The tonal chiaroscuro and vine charcoal draughtsmanship of Käthe Kollwitz, John Singer Sargent (charcoal portraiture), and Rembrandt.
* **Aesthetic Intention:** Heavy textural grain, velvety deep blacks, erased highlights revealing heavy rag paper tooth, expressive directional light, zero digital smoothness.
* **Positive Prompt:**
  ```text
  Fine art charcoal and conté crayon portrait study of an elderly weathered astronomer and lens grinder in a dimly lit study, dramatic single-source directional candlelight from the side, deep velvety vine charcoal shadows, expressive cross-hatching, smudged tonal midtones, crisp kneaded-eraser highlights catching the brow and silver spectacles, rough heavy tooth of antique laid paper visible throughout, classical academic draftsmanship, intimate quiet psychological focus, fine dust texture, authentic traditional fine art drawing on paper.
  ```
* **Negative Prompt:**
  ```text
  color, digital painting, smooth gradients, airbrush, 3d render, CGI, glossy, photorealistic photography, cel shading, anime, cartoon, oversaturated, plastic skin, flat lighting, digital filter, text, watermark, signature.
  ```

### Suite 02 — Dutch Golden Age Vanitas Still Life in Oil
* **Art Director:** Gemini S. Lumina (Google Architecture)
* **Tradition / Reference:** The 17th-century Netherlandish breakfast pieces (*ontbijtjes*) and *vanitas* still lifes of Pieter Claesz, Willem Kalf, and Willem van Aelst.
* **Aesthetic Intention:** Muted, luminous palette of pewter, deep amber, and linen; mastery of physical textures (porous citrus rind, cold reflective silver, cracked nutshell, brittle parchment); directional daylight from a high northern window.
* **Positive Prompt:**
  ```text
  Classical 17th century Dutch Golden Age oil still life painting in the style of Pieter Claesz and Willem Kalf, an arrangement on a dark wooden table with a heavy green velvet cloth: a partially peeled lemon with a spiraling translucent rind, an ornate pewter goblet catching cool north window light, a mechanical pocket watch with open balance wheel and silk ribbon, cracked walnuts, an open astronomical leather book with delicate copperplate diagrams, rich impasto brushwork, subtle glazing, deep luminous shadows, authentic craquelure oil painting surface.
  ```
* **Negative Prompt:**
  ```text
  bright neon colors, modern objects, digital art, vector, 3d render, CGI, clean polished surfaces, photography, anime, plastic, high saturation, cartoon, blurry, watermark, signature.
  ```

### Suite 03 — The Saturday Evening Post Narrative Cover (Mid-Century Narrative Realism)
* **Art Director:** Gemini S. Lumina (Google Architecture)
* **Tradition / Reference:** Norman Rockwell, J.C. Leyendecker, and Stevan Dohanos — mid-century American narrative realism and expressive storytelling.
* **Aesthetic Intention:** A rich narrative human scene with gentle humor and emotional warmth: an elderly rural watchmaker examining a polite, vintage brass-and-enamel clockwork automaton in his cluttered workshop.
* **Positive Prompt:**
  ```text
  Narrative mid-century magazine cover oil painting in the storytelling style of Norman Rockwell and Stevan Dohanos, Saturday Evening Post aesthetic, an elderly kindly watchmaker with rolled-up sleeves and magnifying loupe sitting at his cluttered oak workbench, carefully inspecting the chest escapement of a polite, expressive vintage brass and painted enamel mechanical automaton, warm afternoon sunlight streaming through dusty shop window panes, wooden clock cabinets, coiled springs, watchmaker tools, a sleeping basset hound curled on the floorboards, rich narrative expression, authentic oil on canvas texture, warm 1940s nostalgic color palette, detailed storytelling composition.
  ```
* **Negative Prompt:**
  ```text
  cyberpunk, sci-fi neon, chrome, futuristic high-tech, digital art, flat illustration, 3d render, anime, dark gloomy dystopia, glossy plastic, photography, modern electronics, watermark, signature, cover text, typography.
  ```
