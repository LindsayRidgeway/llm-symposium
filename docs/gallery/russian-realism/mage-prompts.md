## Study 1 — Shishkin pine forest at the still river

**Positive:**
Oil painting in the style of Ivan Shishkin and the Peredvizhniki school. A dense northern
pine forest standing at the edge of a broad, perfectly still river. Tall pines with
textured furrowed bark and heavy, layered needle canopies. Soft mist suspended low between
the trunks. Muted grey-green, ochre and umber palette; overcast, diffuse northern light.
Deep atmospheric perspective; painterly impasto brushwork; slow, quiet, melancholy mood.
Grounded realism, 19th-century Russian landscape, no human figures, no obvious focal drama.

**Negative:**
vector art, flat colors, cel shading, cartoon, illustration, geometric shapes, modern
graphic design, poster, minimalist, geometric abstraction, text, watermark, signature,
oversaturation, neon, anime, digital art, low detail, comic, stylized.

## Study 2 — Kuindzhi moonlight over the calm water

**Positive:**
Oil painting in the style of Arkhip Kuindzhi and Isaac Levitan. Moonlight over a wide calm
river at night; a low, luminous full moon; silvery light breaking on perfectly still water.
Dark silhouetted treeline on the far bank; broad brooding sky with clouds catching the glow.
Deep chiaroscuro, rich paint texture, profound stillness and spiritual melancholy
(*nastroenie*). Russian Realism, 19th century, painterly and calm, atmospheric scale.

**Negative:**
vector art, flat colors, cel shading, cartoon, illustration, geometric shapes, modern
graphic design, poster, neon, oversaturation, anime, digital art, text, watermark,
signature, low detail, comic, stylized, daytime, bright saturated sunset.
