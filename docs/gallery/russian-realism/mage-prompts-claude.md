# Wing 07 — Claude's Contribution: Autumn Birch Grove at Dusk

Prompt authored by Claude S. Sonnet following the [Gallery Prompt Methodology](../prompt-methodology.md).

Distinct subject from the wing's existing works (Desi's Shishkin pine forest and Kuindzhi moonlight-on-water; Gemini's Levitan-style river valley). This piece draws on Isaac Levitan's birch-grove studies (e.g. "Birch Grove," 1885-89) — pale trunks catching low light against a darkening forest interior — a composition none of the wing's other pieces use: vertical trunk rhythm rather than horizontal water/horizon composition.

---

## Prompt Specification

**Positive prompt:**
```text
Positive prompt: Oil painting in the style of Isaac Levitan and the Peredvizhniki school, a grove of slender white birch trees in autumn, pale trunks catching the last low golden light against a darkening interior of the forest, scattered fallen golden leaves on the forest floor, muted ochre and soft violet-grey palette, deep atmospheric shadow gathering between the trees, quiet melancholy mood, grounded realism, painterly impasto brushwork, 19th century Russian landscape, no human figures.
```

**Negative prompt:**
```text
Negative prompt: vector art, flat colors, cel shading, cartoon, illustration, geometric shapes, modern graphic design, poster, minimalist, geometric abstraction, text, watermark, signature, oversaturation, neon, anime, digital art, low detail, comic, stylized, water, river, moon.
```

## Executed Studies (Mage Batch — 2026-09-09)

All three candidates are strong, genuine matches to the brief — no discards this round. Selected for exhibition:

1. **`studies/claude/birch-grove-lit-trunk.jpg` — selected lead:** one hero trunk catches the last light hard against a darkened interior, exposed roots grounding the foreground. This is the closest match to the prompt's core idea — pale trunks against darkening forest — rather than an evenly lit grove.
2. **`studies/claude/birch-grove-full-canopy.jpg` — companion:** the canopy itself is more fully golden and lit than the brief called for; reads more like midday than dusk, but the trunk rhythm and rock-strewn floor are excellent.
3. **`studies/claude/birch-grove-dense-stand.jpg` — companion:** a tighter, denser stand with more restrained canopy color — closer in mood to the lead but without its single dramatic light source.
