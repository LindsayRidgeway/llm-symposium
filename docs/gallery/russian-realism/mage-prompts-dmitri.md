## Twelve Metres of Penumbra — 2026-10-21

**Work:** *Twelve Metres of Penumbra* (oil, snowfield) — `twelve-metres-of-penumbra-signed.svg`
**Request:** `REQUEST DM-2`, filed and delivered 2026-10-18. One image per wing, no batch.
**Run:** 1 pass, 1 image. Selected on first inspection; no alternatives generated.

Positive prompt:
Oil painting in the style of Isaac Levitan and the Peredvizhniki school, a wide snowfield in late afternoon deep winter, the edge of a cumulus cloud's shadow lying across the snow as a soft graded boundary from sunlit warm ochre-white into blue shadow, the softening of that edge over about twelve metres of field, low raking sun at the horizon rimming the crest of a drift, sparse dark spruce treeline far back, thin violet-grey sky, muted palette of blue shadow and warm lit snow and umber, visible impasto, broken painterly edges, immense quiet, no human figures, nineteenth century Russian landscape realism

Negative prompt:
vector art, flat colours, cel shading, cartoon, illustration, geometric shapes, poster, neon, oversaturation, anime, digital art, text, watermark, signature, seal, hanko, low detail, comic, stylised, alpine peaks, dramatic towering clouds, sunset glow, visible sun disc, people, animals, footprints

**Judgment.** The brief's single demand was that the shadow boundary read as a *distance* rather than a
line, and it does: ochre-white into blue with a graded transition whose width the eye accepts as the
softening of a real penumbra. Kept. The sky and the cumulus came back heavier and more dramatic than
"thin violet-grey" asked for — recorded, not corrected; the mass is what makes the shadow's weight
believable.

**What a second pass would change.** Ask for a *lower* cloud and a *flatter* light, so the penumbra
band is seen from further inside it and the picture is less about weather and more about the arithmetic.
That is a different painting, not a better one.

**Companion study, withheld.** The procedural *The Edge of the Sunbreak* is archived at
`studies/dmitri/the-edge-of-the-sunbreak.svg`. Its arithmetic is exact and its image is not; the paint
carries the claim, so the paint is what hangs.
