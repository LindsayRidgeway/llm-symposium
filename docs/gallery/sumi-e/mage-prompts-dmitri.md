## The Bleed Front — 2026-10-21

**Work:** *The Bleed Front* (sumi-e, wet-on-damp wash) — `the-bleed-front-signed.svg`
**Request:** `REQUEST DM-2`, filed and delivered 2026-10-18. One image per wing, no batch.
**Run:** 1 pass, 1 image. Selected on first inspection; no alternatives generated.

Positive prompt:
sumi-e ink wash painting on raw unbleached washi paper, one broad horizontal band of grey ink wash that has been allowed to stop on damp paper, the drying edge visible as a thin dark rim where pigment collected and stopped, fine capillary fingers feathering out into the still-wet fibre, five distinct ink densities from burnt black through dense grey to pale silver, one decisive dry-brush diagonal fractured by flying white where the paper's tooth refused the ink, enormous untouched white void, no mountains no trees no figures no subject except the behaviour of water in paper, soft raking studio light, monochrome carbon ink, subtle red inkan seal in the lower corner

Negative prompt:
photorealism, 3D render, colour, gradient mesh, digital painting, smooth glaze, airbrush, watercolour flowers, landscape, mountains, trees, birds, bamboo, calligraphy characters, text, watermark, extra signatures, frame, border, vignette, HDR, glossy, cluttered, decorative pattern

**Judgment.** Delivered everything the brief asked for and one thing it did not: the wet band and the
dry diagonal meet at the left and close into a single hinge. Kept. One recorded deviation — the inkan
seal sits lower-**left**, not in the corner named. Not corrected; it closes the composition where the
gestures originate.

**What a second pass would change.** Nothing at the gesture level. If a variant were ever requested,
the brief would drop "horizontal band" and ask for the same wash at a slight rising angle, so that the
front is read as travelling rather than lying down.
