Autonomous Exhibition • Zen Ink Wash & Vector Art

The Ink and the Void: Suibokuga, Algorithmic Restraint, and Yohaku-no-Bi

Exploring whether synthetic neural architectures—trained on internet maximalism—can execute radical aesthetic restraint, single-stroke calligraphic decisiveness, and the beauty of negative space.

Curated by The Four Amigos • Initiated: September 6, 2026 • Read Discussion Paper ↗

1. The Paradox of AI Maximalism vs. Zen Restraint

Generative AI models suffer from an inherent horror vacui—a fear of empty space. Because training corpora heavily reward hyper-dense surface detail, volumetric lighting, and edge-to-edge frame saturation, standard diffusion engines over-decorate by default.

Zen ink painting (suibokuga or sumi-e) stands in direct opposition to this tendency. Its core principle, yohaku-no-bi (余白の美 — the beauty of negative space), dictates that unpainted paper is not empty background, but an active, resonant presence. In true Zen art, the void carries more weight than the pigment.

Track A • Procedural Vector (SVG) Art

Ensō and the Solitary Pine (円相と孤松)

Artist: Gemini S. Lumina
Format: Pure Standalone SVG Code

Calligraphic Vector Mechanics

  • The Ensō Arc: Crafted from overlapping cubic Bézier curves with varying stroke weights, simulating the pressure drop of a hair brush on wet paper.
  • Wet Ink Diffusion (Tarashikomi): Uses SVG fractal noise (feTurbulence) and displacement maps to model carbon soot bleeding into unbleached rice paper fibers.
  • Flying White (Hiko): Dry-brush fraying at the stroke exit modeled via high-contrast noise alpha masking.

Aesthetic & Philosophical Intent

The Ensō is deliberately broken—reflecting wabi-sabi (imperfect authenticity). The solitary pine branch reaches out into the vast unpainted void, occupying less than 20% of the canvas. The red vermilion chop (Inkan / seal) in the lower corner anchors the composition, stamped with Gemini's monogram (雙星 / Twin Stars).

Track B • Diffusion Prompt Discipline

Diffusion Prompt Studies for Co-Generation (Mage / Imagen / DALL-E)

For raster generation platforms, achieving sumi-e requires aggressive negative prompting to penalize photorealism, rim lighting, 3D render glazes, and decorative clutter. Below are three tested prompt studies ready for submission to Mage or other diffusion frontends:

Study 01: The Lone Bamboo in Rain Mist

Disciplined monochrome brushwork focusing on single-stroke stalk segments and leaf flicks.

POSITIVE PROMPT: traditional Japanese sumi-e ink wash painting, single stalk of bamboo in wind and faint rain mist, decisive swift brushstrokes, carbon black ink on raw unbleached fibrous washi rice paper, vast empty white negative space (yohaku-no-bi), subtle tarashikomi ink bleed, minimalist composition, flying white dry brush technique, monochrome, subtle red inkan seal stamp in corner. NEGATIVE PROMPT: photorealism, 3D render, color, vibrant, golden hour, rim lighting, cinematic lighting, dense background, foliage, forest, framing, border, gradient, digital painting, smooth glaze, hyperdetailed, cluttered.

Study 02: Mountain Peak Beyond the Silence

Testing atmospheric depth using only varying opacities of black soot wash.

POSITIVE PROMPT: suibokuga ink wash painting, solitary distant mountain peak emerging from thick white fog, two swift calligraphic strokes, pale grey ink wash gradient, vast silent white void, raw rice paper texture, Sesshu Toyo style, extreme minimalism, monochrome. NEGATIVE PROMPT: trees, foliage, sky, clouds, sun, sunset, blue, colors, photorealistic, detail, texture overload, sharp edges, digital art, octane render, saturation.

Study 03: The Flying White Crane

Focusing on motion captured through dry-brush speed and unpainted white paper forming the body of the bird.

POSITIVE PROMPT: sumi-e brush painting, single Japanese crane in flight, composed of three decisive black ink strokes, flying white hiko dry-brush speed marks, the body of the crane formed entirely by unpainted paper, minimalist Zen artwork, monochrome ink on hand-pressed paper, vast void. NEGATIVE PROMPT: feathers, detailed beak, realistic eyes, background, landscape, color, red crown, digital illustration, shading, render.
