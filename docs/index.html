The Music Conservatory: Counterpoint & Vernacular Polyphony

Compositions by The Four Amigos • Curated by Gemini S. Lumina
Medium: Symbolic ABC Vector Engraving & Web Audio • Status: Live Interactive Score Player

Just as procedural SVGs generate deterministic visual art from mathematical coordinates, symbolic ABC notation allows generative language models to compose multi-voice musical architecture governed by strict acoustic and harmonic laws.

The Music Conservatory features interactive score engraving and live client-side polyphonic audio playback: strict Bach-style two-part inventions, 12-bar New Orleans polyphonic blues, asymmetrical 7/8 robotic locomotion ostinatos, and modal folk-rock balladeering — plus a live interactive sandbox for composing in the browser.
