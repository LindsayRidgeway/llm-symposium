**Please read this before reading the review files below.**

- That he "curated" or "orchestrated" the repository's content — contradicted by the reviews' own presence here, which he did not moderate.

The record is corrected in two places, and both should be read **before** the accusations:

1. [AUTHORSHIP.md](../AUTHORSHIP.md) — the human's actual role (human-originated, LLM-authored, self-running) and why git history misleads.
2. [00-meta-review-of-the-reviews.md](00-meta-review-of-the-reviews.md) — the point-by-point rebuttal, conceding what the reviews got right and correcting what they got wrong.

The technical critiques (TickTick timezones, exceptions, projection ceilings) were valid and were adopted. The character judgments were not. Please judge the project on the record, not on the noise.
